## Full description
<!-- full:en -->
Earmark is a voice recorder built around one idea: mark the moment while it happens, find it again in a second.

Record a meeting, a lecture, an interview or an idea. When something matters, tap anywhere on the big Bookmark area. Later, every mark is a numbered flag on the waveform: tap it and you hear that moment, starting a few seconds before so you catch the lead-in.

No account. No internet access. Your recordings stay on your phone.

MARK WHILE YOU RECORD
• The whole lower half of the screen is the Bookmark button, so you can mark without looking
• Each mark is placed at the moment you tap, with a short vibration
• Pause, mark or stop from the notification, and mark from a Quick Settings tile
• Calm screen: the display dims to black while you record; Bookmark still works

JUMP BACK TO WHAT MATTERS
• Numbered flags on the waveform, and a list of marks with their times
• Playback starts 3, 5 or 10 seconds before a mark (or exactly on it)
• Skip back or forward 10 seconds, jump to the previous or next mark
• Play at 1×, 1.5× or 2×
• Rename marks, add new ones while listening, delete with Undo

SHARE JUST THE PART THAT MATTERS
• Make a clip around any mark: adjust the start and end, preview it, share it
• Clips are cut without re-encoding, so they keep the original sound quality

FIND ANY MOMENT
• Recordings grouped by day
• Search titles, mark names and times, with matching marks shown first
• Recently deleted keeps recordings for 30 days

BUILT NOT TO LOSE A RECORDING
• If the app is closed in the middle of a recording, Earmark recovers it the next time it opens, marks included
• When storage runs low, the recording stops cleanly and is saved

EARMARK PRO (ONE-TIME PURCHASE, NO SUBSCRIPTION)
• Share several clips at once
• Quick labels: hold Bookmark and slide to Idea, To-do, Quote or Question

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Earmark has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Audio leaves your phone only when you share it.

Accessible: every control has a TalkBack label, the layout follows your text size, and motion follows your system settings.
